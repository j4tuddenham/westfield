Things to do around Ullswater.

Beyond the lake

Things to do around Ullswater.

Westfield's position in Watermillock puts you within easy reach of the water, the fells and a handful of Cumbria's best family days out — as well as Penrith for a wider choice of shops and restaurants. For the lake itself and its steamers, see our Ullswater guide; for routes on foot, see walking.

01 · Watersports

Kayaking, sailing & paddleboarding

Ullswater Paddleboarding is based right in Watermillock. Further round the lake, Glenridding Sailing Centre, Ullswater Yacht Club and St Patrick's Boat Landing offer sailing tuition, kayaks, canoes and boat hire.

02 · Historic house

Dalemain Mansion & Gardens

Medieval, Tudor and Georgian architecture, award-winning gardens and the home of the World's Original Marmalade Awards, a short drive towards Penrith.

03 · Ruins & gardens

Lowther Castle & Gardens

Dramatic castle ruins, gardens-within-gardens and one of the UK's largest adventure playgrounds, near Penrith.

04 · All-weather

Rheged

A visitor centre built into a Penrith hillside with a giant-screen cinema, galleries, and Cumbrian food and shopping — a good option on a wet day.

05 · Wildlife

Red squirrels at Aira Force

The woodland around Aira Force is one of the more reliable places in Cumbria to spot red squirrels, alongside the waterfall itself.

06 · Cycling

Quiet lanes & the C2C

The lanes around Ullswater and the Eden Valley make for good road cycling, and the C2C (Sea to Sea) route passes through the wider Penrith area.
