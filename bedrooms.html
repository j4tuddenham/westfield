Where you'll sleep at Westfield.

A king room, a twin room and a double room — comfortable space for families and groups exploring Ullswater.

Where you'll sleep

Three bedrooms, two bathrooms, space for six.

Westfield sleeps up to six guests across three bedrooms, making it a comfortable fit for two families holidaying together, a group of friends, or a multi-generational trip to Ullswater. The cottage is arranged predominantly over one level, with a short flight of three steps between the kitchen/dining area and two of the bedrooms.

Bedroom 01

The King Room

1 king-size bed. Set apart from the other two bedrooms, on its own side of the cottage, for a quieter and more private space — a good choice for the earliest sleeper in the group, or as the "owners' bedroom" for a family holiday.

Bedroom 02

The Twin Room

2 single beds, ideal for two friends, siblings, or older children. Reached via a short flight of steps from the kitchen and dining area, alongside the double room.

Bedroom 03

The Double Room

1 double bed, situated alongside the twin room on the same side of the cottage — well suited to a couple travelling as part of a larger group.

Bathrooms

Two bathrooms for six guests.

Westfield has two bathrooms shared between the three bedrooms, so mornings before a day on the lake or the fells don't mean queuing. For a full run-through of the rest of the cottage — kitchen, living room, terrace and hot tub — see the cottage page, and browse the gallery for photos of each room.
