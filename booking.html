Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

What time is check-in and check-out?

Check-in is from 15:00. Your exact check-out time is confirmed with your booking — check your confirmation email or get in touch if you're unsure.

How many guests can Westfield sleep?

Up to six guests, across three bedrooms — a king room, a twin room and a double room — with two bathrooms. See the full bedroom details.

Does Westfield have a hot tub?

Yes — a private hot tub on the terrace, available to guests year-round. See the cottage for more.

Is there parking?

Yes, free private parking on site, including an EV charge point.

Are pets welcome?

Please message us on WhatsApp before booking if you're hoping to bring a pet, so we can confirm this for your dates.

Is the cottage accessible for guests with limited mobility?

The cottage is arranged predominantly over one level, with three steps between the kitchen/dining area and two of the bedrooms. Take a look at the gallery and get in touch with any specific questions before booking.

What's nearby?

Ullswater, the Ullswater Way, and a good choice of pubs are all close by — see things to do, walking and food & drink for more.
