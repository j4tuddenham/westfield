About the cottage

A traditional Lakeland holiday cottage in Watermillock.

Westfield is a traditional Lakeland stone holiday cottage in Watermillock, near Ullswater in Cumbria, offering comfortable, well-equipped accommodation for up to six guests. Three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a separate living room and a spacious kitchen and dining area give families and groups of friends plenty of room to relax together.

The living room opens toward the outdoor terrace — a relaxed spot for evenings in after a day exploring. The kitchen opens into the dining area, creating a sociable space to gather, cook and plan the next day's adventure.

Set within a traditional Lakeland hamlet, Westfield keeps its mature planting, stone walls and characterful stone-and-slate architecture — a peaceful, practical base with the charm of an older cottage intact. The layout is arranged predominantly over one level, with three steps between the kitchen/dining area and two of the bedrooms; guests are encouraged to check the photographs before booking.

Location

Holiday cottage in Watermillock, near Ullswater.

Westfield is in the quiet hamlet of Bennett Head, Watermillock, within the Lake District National Park — close to Ullswater and the Ullswater Way, and a practical base for exploring the lake, local footpaths and the surrounding fells.

Watermillock sits on the quieter side of Ullswater, within easy reach of Penrith and the M6 — an accessible base that still feels tucked away from the busier parts of the Lake District.

For guests planning a Lake District holiday, Westfield combines a traditional Lakeland setting with comfortable three-bedroom accommodation for up to six people.
