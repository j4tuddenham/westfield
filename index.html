About the cottage

A traditional Lakeland holiday cottage in Watermillock.

Westfield is a traditional Lakeland stone holiday cottage in Watermillock, near Ullswater in Cumbria, offering comfortable, well-equipped accommodation for up to six guests across three bedrooms and two bathrooms.

Set within a traditional Lakeland hamlet, Westfield keeps its mature planting, stone walls and characterful stone-and-slate architecture — a peaceful, practical base with the charm of an older cottage intact.
