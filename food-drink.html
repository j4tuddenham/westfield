Watermillock & Pooley Bridge

Food & drink near Ullswater.

From a fireside pub dinner in Watermillock to a lakeside restaurant table — good food is close by.

In Watermillock

Two good options, a short walk or drive away.

The Brackenrigg Inn, on the A592 in Watermillock, is a refurbished 18th-century coaching inn with a sun-trap terrace looking out over Ullswater to the fells beyond. Expect hearty, locally sourced pub food — steak and ale pie, fish and chips, Sunday roasts — local ales, and a warm, dog-friendly welcome.

Another Place, The Lake, also in Watermillock, is a design-led lakeside hotel with the Rampsbeck Restaurant, an all-day café and lake-view dining. Non-residents are welcome to book for dinner, and the hotel also runs wild swimming and paddleboard sessions from its jetty.

Pooley Bridge

A short drive to the northern shore.

Sun Inn

A traditional village pub in the centre of Pooley Bridge, popular with walkers finishing the Ullswater Way.

Crown Inn

Another long-standing Pooley Bridge pub, a short stroll from the northern steamer pier.

Pooley Bridge Inn

Riverside pub and rooms right by the bridge over the River Eamont.

1863 Bar & Restaurant

A more contemporary dining option on Pooley Bridge's high street.

Further afield

Worth the extra few minutes.

Heading towards Penrith, the Horse & Farrier at Dacre and the Herdwick Inn at Penruddock are both well-regarded countryside pubs. Dalemain's Great Barn Tearoom serves light lunches and cakes alongside the gardens (see our Ullswater guide), and Penrith itself has a wider range of cafés, restaurants and takeaways for an evening out.
