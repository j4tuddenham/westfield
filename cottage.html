Bennett Head, Watermillock

A traditional Lakeland cottage, with a hot tub of its own.

Three bedrooms, two bathrooms and space for six — a stone-and-slate holiday cottage built for slow mornings and long evenings on the terrace.

About the cottage

Comfortable, well-equipped, and built for groups.

Westfield is a traditional Lakeland stone holiday cottage in Watermillock, near Ullswater in Cumbria, offering comfortable, well-equipped accommodation for up to six guests. Three bedrooms, two bathrooms, a separate living room and a spacious kitchen and dining area give families and groups of friends plenty of room to relax together — see the full bedroom layout.

The living room opens toward the outdoor terrace — a relaxed spot for evenings in after a day exploring. The kitchen opens into the dining area, creating a sociable space to gather, cook and plan the next day's adventure on the lake or the fells.

Set within a traditional Lakeland hamlet, Westfield keeps its mature planting, stone walls and characterful stone-and-slate architecture — a peaceful, practical base with the charm of an older cottage intact.

Layout & accessibility

Mostly single-level, with a short flight of steps.

The cottage is arranged predominantly over one level, with three steps between the kitchen/dining area and two of the bedrooms (the twin room and the double room). The King Room sits apart from the other two bedrooms, giving one guest or couple a quieter, more private corner of the cottage.

We'd encourage anyone with mobility needs to look through the gallery before booking, or get in touch via WhatsApp with any specific questions about the layout, parking or access.
