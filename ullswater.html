Watermillock, Ullswater

England's most beautiful lake, on your doorstep.

Steamers, waterfalls, historic estates and quiet villages — everything Ullswater, a short walk or drive from Westfield.

England's most beautiful lake

About Ullswater.

Ullswater is the second-largest lake in the Lake District, a glacial, "Z"-shaped ribbon of water stretching roughly nine miles from Pooley Bridge in the north to Glenridding and Patterdale in the south. Gently rolling farmland around Pooley Bridge gives way, further south, to steep fellsides that plunge into the water — culminating in Helvellyn, England's third-highest mountain.

It was on the shore near Glencoyne Bay, close to Aira Force, that William Wordsworth saw the drifts of daffodils that inspired his poem "I Wandered Lonely as a Cloud" — one of several literary and historic threads that run through the valley.

On the water

Ullswater Steamers.

Heritage vessels

The Ullswater Navigation & Transit Company has sailed the lake since 1859. Two of today's vessels, Raven (1889) and Lady of the Lake (1877), are among the oldest working passenger vessels in the world.

Four piers

Cruises call at Glenridding, Pooley Bridge, Howtown and the Aira Force shuttle pier, so you can hop on and off to explore each stop.

Walkers' tickets

A popular option is to sail one way — Glenridding to Howtown is a favourite — and walk back along the shore, described by many as one of the finest lakeside walks in Lakeland.

Around the lake

Aira Force, Dalemain, and the villages.

Aira Force is a 65-70ft waterfall in a wooded valley on the northern shore, managed by the National Trust, with trails weaving from the lakeshore up to Gowbarrow Fell past red squirrels, towering firs and picnic spots. It's one of the most-visited beauty spots on the lake, and a natural pairing with the Aira Force steamer shuttle from Glenridding.

Dalemain Mansion & Historic Gardens, near Pooley Bridge and Penrith, is a working family estate with medieval, Tudor and Georgian architecture, award-winning gardens, and the World's Original Marmalade Awards. The Dalemain Loop is a gentle circular walk taking in Dacre village and Pooley Bridge.

Pooley Bridge, Glenridding, Patterdale and Howtown are the main villages ringing the lake, each with a different character — see our full location guide for how they connect to Westfield.

On the water

Watersports at Watermillock.

Ullswater Paddleboarding is based at The Sheep Shed in Watermillock itself, on the western shore — a short drive from Westfield — offering guided paddleboarding and kayaking sessions on the lake. Further round the shore, Glenridding Sailing Centre and the Ullswater Yacht Club's Sailing School (near Pooley Bridge) offer sailing tuition and boat hire, while St Patrick's Boat Landing and Lakeland Boat Hire rent motor boats and rowing boats.
